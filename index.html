The problem

Workforce Demographics Dashboard Internal

The problem

Records for everyone ever hired on contract (3,000+ people) sat in a multi-sheet Excel masterlist. Questions like who leaves, how quickly and where they live took hours of manual filtering.

What I built

An interactive dashboard that turns a masterlist upload into charts for employment status, department, age and generation, gender, production process, education, joining date, home state and tenure at resignation. Clicking any chart filters the whole page down to the matching people. Uploads are cleaned automatically, and the previous upload is kept for one-click revert.
